I am a PhD Candidate in Finance at **Imperial Business School** and a PhD Intern in the Stress-Testing Division at the **Federal Reserve Board**.

My research focuses on **empirical banking, financial intermediation, and real estate finance**. I study how financial regulation shapes bank lending decisions and credit allocation, combining structural modeling with administrative microdata to quantify mechanisms and evaluate policy counterfactuals.

I will be on the 2026–2027 academic job market and available for interviews.
