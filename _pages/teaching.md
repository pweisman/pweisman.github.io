### Courses Taught

#### **Imperial Business School**
* **Big Data in Finance I** *(Master's, 2024–2025)* — Teaching Assistant for Prof. Tarun Ramadorai  
  Led Python and R quantitative computing labs covering supervised learning (random forests, boosting, LASSO), neural networks, cross-validation, and ROC curves. Guided applications to credit analytics (corporate, retail, and mortgage default prediction), asset management (stock return prediction, factor models), text/NLP for portfolio construction, and algorithmic bias in fintech lending.
* **Big Data in Finance II** *(Master's, 2024)* — Teaching Assistant for Prof. Ansgar Walther  
  Supervised graduate team projects applying machine learning and reinforcement learning to automated trading strategies, hedge fund portfolio choice, and credit screening models.

#### **London School of Economics (LSE)**
* **Financial Markets and Portfolio Management** *(Undergraduate, 2023)* — Teaching Assistant for Dr. Walker Ray and Dr. Linyan Zhu  
  Led tutorials and empirical quantitative labs for an intensive three-week summer course covering optimal portfolio selection, CAPM/consumption CAPM, multi-factor models (APT, I-CAPM), Fama-MacBeth regressions, return predictability tests, behavioral finance, and bond portfolio management.
